Case # JIH-AA-0001
*Building: Juniper Island Hospital*
*Report by: Dr. Grigory Ivanov*

Issue: Strange Happenings


Content: The thoracic vertebrae does not bend backward 330 degrees. Avid fanatics of Juniper Circus may disagree, but contortionists achieve those feats of flexibility solely by the functioning of their cervical vertebrae and some give on the lumbar area. Meaning that our neck and lower back allow that characteristic bend of the spine, where nothing breaks if well trained. That is not what I am referring to when I witness to how my first patient of the day bent the middle of her spine 330 degrees backward without breaking her thorax open, unhinged her jaw, and started swallowing herself feet-first in my office. I find this type of behavior extremely rude. Had she not wanted to respond my query into her drug-related history, she could have merely informed me of such. 

Nonetheless, this scene became the catalyst for me to witness one of the so called —strange happenings— occurring throughout Juniper Island, for the moment I stepped out of my office to inform my secretary of the patient's macroautophagy, I happened to notice one of the men in the waiting room was not smiling. Not a grin, not a smirk, not even a tug up on his lips, if anything I would say the man appeared to express absolute horror. Needless to say, my secretary was as dumbfounded as I was. Even my latter patient had engulfed her own skull with a joyful countenance, there was no reason for this man not to muster up a pleasant expression. Of course I did nothing about this, his name was never called into my office, and he naturally left once the sun began to set. 

I understand our island has pivoted towards tourism in an effort to revitalize our failing economy, still I am tired of these foreigners messing with our way of life. As such I invite any and all hospital employees to add their complaints to this catalogue which will be later used to pressure our mayoral office into restoring our peaceful tradition of isolation and bliss.
May we all stay ignorant.


*Witness Note: The unsmiling man was later apprehended for disturbance of the peace. Court date still pending.*
